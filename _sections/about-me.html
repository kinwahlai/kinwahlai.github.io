About Me

A Certified Satir CMP coach and trainer-in-training, I offer over 15 years of leadership and software development experience gained across 4 countries. With an attentive and compassionate approach, I help individuals achieve personal and professional growth.

My Mission

I'm dedicated to helping people cultivate self-awareness, strengthen relationships, and foster inner peace. My goal is to create a safe space where clients can explore challenges, discover insights, and develop practical tools for lasting transformation.

My Journey

My path to coaching began through my own personal growth journey. After experiencing the profound impact of the Satir Model in my life, I became passionate about sharing these transformative tools with others to help them navigate their unique challenges.
